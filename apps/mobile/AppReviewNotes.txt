2. APP PURPOSE
Problem it solves: Instagram creators and small businesses get flooded with keyword comments (e.g. "guide", "link") on their Reels and can't reply to each one with the right DM fast enough.
Value it provides: InstaAuto watches a creator's own connected Instagram account for comments matching chosen keywords and automatically sends the matching DM reply (and optionally a public comment reply), so the creator never manually copy-pastes the same response hundreds of times.

3. ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS AND TEST CREDENTIALS
Steps to reach the core features:
1. Launch the app and sign in with the test account below.
2. The Dashboard shows a demo-connected Instagram account with real automations, message, and comment history already populated — no further setup is required to see the app in use.
3. Tap "Automations" to view/create a keyword-triggered auto-reply rule on a Reel.
4. Tap "Messages" or "Comments" to view the inbox of Instagram interactions.
5. Tap "Settings" → "Instagram accounts" to see the connect/disconnect flow, and "Delete account" to see in-app account deletion.
