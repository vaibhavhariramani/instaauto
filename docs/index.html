FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a Facebook Page to connect Instagram?

No. InstaAuto connects directly to your Instagram professional account.

Can I run automations on more than one account?

Yes — connect multiple Instagram accounts and switch between them from Settings.

What happens to my data if I delete my account?

Deleting your account in Settings permanently removes your data. See our data deletion policy for details.

Does InstaAuto post on my behalf without my input?

Only within the rules you set — a keyword, a Reel, and a message you write yourself. Nothing sends until you configure it.
